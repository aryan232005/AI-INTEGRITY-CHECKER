A circular economy aims to keep products and materials in use for as long as possible by designing out waste. Strategies include repair, refurbishment, remanufacturing and recycling, and replacing disposable items with reusable or compostable alternatives. Proponents argue that it reduces dependence on virgin resources and exposure to price swings in commodities. Critics note that recycling is energy-intensive, that composting requires proper collection infrastructure, and that claims of compostability are often hard for consumers to verify.
